PRIVACY

Sons of Thunder is a Winter Garden brotherhood. This page is for the Thunder Board at sonsofthunder.netlify.app.

What we keep

If you lock a Monday seat, we may keep your first name, phone number, optional email, and optional birthday. We use that to hold your seat, text you about the gathering, and honor a birthday if you asked us to.

Your number

We do not sell your mobile number. We do not share mobile numbers with other organizations, advertisers, or lists.

Texts

Message frequency is about one text when you lock a seat, plus occasional gathering notes. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

Questions

Ask a brother who brought you, or use the board’s text-a-leader path.
