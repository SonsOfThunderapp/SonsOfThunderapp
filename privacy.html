PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This is the official privacy policy for Sons of Thunder (Winter Garden, Florida) and the Thunder Board SMS program at https://sonsofthunderboard.com/.

Who we are

Sons of Thunder is a local men’s brotherhood. Thunder Board is our private board for the next gathering. We are not a marketing list, affiliate program, or lead-generation business.

What we collect and why

If you choose to lock a seat or opt in to SMS, we may collect: first name, mobile phone number, optional email, and optional birthday (month and day only). We use that information to hold your seat, send the SMS program you opted into, and honor a birthday only if you asked.

SMS program

Program name: Sons of Thunder SMS. Messages are about your locked-in gathering seat, gathering reminders, and a birthday honor if you opted in. Message frequency varies (typically 1–4 messages per month). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of membership or of using Thunder Board.

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information and messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We do not sell personal information. We do not buy or rent phone lists. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How you opt in

Opt-in happens only on Thunder Board at https://sonsofthunderboard.com/. You type your own mobile number and check a box that is never pre-checked. That consent is for this SMS program only. It is not bundled into another agreement.

How to reach us

Privacy questions: use Thunder Board at https://sonsofthunderboard.com/ or ask the host in the room.
