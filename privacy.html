PRIVACY POLICY

Sons of Thunder is a Winter Garden, Florida brotherhood. This privacy policy covers the Thunder Board at https://sonsofthunder.netlify.app/ and the SMS program tied to it.

Who we are

We are a local brotherhood for ordinary men. The board holds the next gathering and the room. It is not a church plant, not a radio ministry, and not a marketing list.

What personal information we collect and why

If you lock a Monday seat or opt in to texts, we may collect your first name, mobile phone number, optional email address, and optional birthday. We use that personal information to hold your seat, operate the SMS program, send gathering notes you asked for, and honor a birthday only if you asked us to.

SMS and mobile data

If you check the text box on the board, you are opting in to program texts from Sons of Thunder. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help.

Mobile opt-in information and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with other organizations, advertisers, affiliates, or lists for their marketing.

How to reach us about privacy

Privacy questions go through Thunder Board at https://sonsofthunder.netlify.app/. Ask the host on the board, or ask the brother who brought you.

See also our SMS Terms.
