WHO WE ARE

Jesus named James and John Boanerges - Sons of Thunder (Mark 3:17) - wild men who wanted to call down fire. That's us: men who want to lead, know we can be hotheads, and need brothers who keep us sharp.

We gather for mentorship, stories, a drink, the occasional cigar - and for the range, the lake, the Word, and the gym. Ages 21-75. One aim: be better men where it counts - marriage, family, work, friends, neighbors. Looking more like Jesus, the ultimate gentleman.
