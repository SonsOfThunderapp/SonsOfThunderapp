SMS TERMS AND CONDITIONS

These terms cover the Sons of Thunder SMS program for the Winter Garden brotherhood, sent through Thunder Board at https://sonsofthunderboard.com/.

How you opt in

You opt in only on the board. You enter your own mobile number and check a box that is not pre-checked: yes, text me once about the birthday honor. That is the only way we start texts. No purchase is required.

What we send

Program texts about your locked-in Monday seat, a birthday honor if you asked for it, and occasional notes about the next gathering. Not ads. Not a blast list. Not a radio or sales campaign.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You may receive about one text when you lock a seat, then occasional gathering notes.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Your carrier’s rates are yours.

STOP and HELP

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help. After STOP we will not text that number again unless you opt in again on the board.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Mobile opt-in information and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Full details: https://sonsofthunderboard.com/privacy.
