SMS TERMS

These terms cover texts from the Winter Garden Sons of Thunder brotherhood through Thunder Board.

How you opt in

On sonsofthunder.netlify.app you enter your own number and check a box that is not pre-checked: yes, text me once about the birthday honor. That is the only way we start texts.

What we send

One confirmation / honor text after you lock a seat, with a link to add a birthday if you want it. Occasional notes about the next Monday gathering. Not ads. Not a blast list.

Rates and frequency

About one message per signup, then occasional gathering notes. Message and data rates may apply.

Stop and help

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help. After STOP we will not text that number again unless you opt in on the board.

Privacy

Mobile numbers are not shared. See Privacy.
